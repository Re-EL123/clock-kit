HOW IT WORKS

From clock-in to clock-out.

Five honest steps — no spreadsheets, no WhatsApp forwards.

  1. Sign in

    A one-time code by SMS or email confirms it's really you.

  2. Clock in at the site

    Location is checked against the site geofence, then the clock is running.

  3. Work, breaks, done

    Start break, end break, clock out — each moment is captured, even offline.

  4. Host reviews

    Attendance is reviewed, corrections are rare, and the timesheet is approval-signed.

  5. Settled & billed

    The signed timesheet flows to billing and payroll — one clean source of truth.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do my workers need a smartphone or an app?

A phone with any browser is enough — there's no app store download. Workers sign in with a one-time code by SMS or email and the clock-in page loads like a website.

What happens when there's load-shedding or no signal?

Clock-actions are saved on the device and shown as PENDING SYNC until the moment goes through to the server. Nothing is guessed and nothing is lost.

Now would you prevent buddy punching?

Clocks are tied to a signed-in person, site geofences limit where a clock-in can happen, and the server records the authoritative time for every action.

Can workers clock in for each other?

No. Each clock belongs to a verified session, and anything that looks unusual is visible to the host and the agency for review — with corrections tracked.

How do timesheets and billing work?

Reviewed attendance rolls straight into a timesheet the host signs. Signed timesheets become the basis for billing — one clean, dispute-free source of truth.
